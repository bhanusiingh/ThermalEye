      Five stages from heat signal to context

      Each stage adds context. None of them, alone or together, turns a thermal anomaly into a confirmed fire.

      1. Thermal ingestion

        NASA VIIRS near-real-time satellite detections.

      2. Spatio-temporal clustering

        ST-DBSCAN groups nearby detections in space and time into candidate events.

      3. Infrastructure context

        OpenStreetMap proximity analysis and mapped facility information.

      4. Multispectral analysis

        Sentinel-2 RGB and SWIR imagery around the event.

      5. Land-cover classification

        MobileNetV2 describes surrounding land cover. It does not confirm fire.

      What to keep in mind

      • Thermal anomalies are not automatically confirmed fires.
      • Sentinel-2 is contextual imagery, not active-fire temperature confirmation.
      • OpenStreetMap coverage may be incomplete.
      • Machine-learning predictions have limitations.
      • Missing evidence is displayed as unavailable.
